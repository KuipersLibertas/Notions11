## LinkedIn

**Post**

A question I see constantly from Notion creators: "How do I add a password to my Notion page before sharing it?"

The short answer: Notion doesn't support it natively.

When you turn on "Share to web" in Notion, your page becomes fully public. Anyone with the link can open it — no sign-in, no gate. The only built-in alternative is inviting people individually by email, which requires each recipient to have a Notion account.

For freelancers sharing client deliverables, educators distributing course materials, or creators selling Notion templates, neither option is great.

There are three practical approaches:

1. **Invite-only** — works if everyone has a Notion account. Doesn't scale for template sales or public distribution.

3. **Super.so or Potion** — full site builders with password options, better if you also want a custom domain and design control.
